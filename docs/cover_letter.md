# Cover letter draft — Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine

Dear Editor,

Please consider “SPARTA: dual graph operators for modelling immune-cell migration and IgG-sized transport barriers from spatial transcriptomics” for publication as an original research article in *Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine*.

SPARTA formulates two distinct transport questions on a shared spatial-transcriptomics graph. A source–sink minimum-cut operator returns a model-defined cellular bottleneck and its cut geometry; a screened diffusion–absorption operator returns a model-defined field for an IgG-sized molecule. We apply these operators to 19 primary tissue sections from seven individuals and assess the per-section association between the resulting fields, a selection-matched counterfactual, input-availability sensitivity, and the effect of disconnected nodes. Three additional public sections are analysed separately as an exploratory external arm.

The manuscript presents SPARTA as a computational method demonstration. The outputs are not measurements of antibody exposure, T-cell migration, tissue permeability, treatment response, or clinical risk. The primary cohort contains repeated sections from a small number of individuals, the external arm contains only three sections from two individuals, and the transport assumptions have not been calibrated against functional measurements. The manuscript reports these limitations and does not claim clinical prediction or superiority over spatial-domain methods.

We believe the work is relevant to CMPB because it defines explicit, reproducible graph operators for biomedical spatial data and examines how their assumptions affect interpretation. Source code and analysis outputs are being assembled into a versioned archive; the final data-availability statement and citation will be updated with the verified DOI before submission.
